Media, text and recipe parity

Findings and proposals for @crystal-ui/core and Crystal React, answering Meridian's brief of 2 October: too few recipes to keep platforms consistent, hidden animations, inconsistent material composition, players without their padding and features, and a text element without a vocabulary. Every page here loads the library from core/assets by relative path and adds layout only. Serve the repository with node proposals/2026-10-02-media-text-and-recipe-parity/examples/serve.cjs.
