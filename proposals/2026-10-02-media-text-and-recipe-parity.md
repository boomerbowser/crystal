# Media, text and recipe parity

2 October 2026. A review of `@crystal-ui/core` and Crystal React against the
brief Meridian gave on that date, with the findings, what was built, the
decisions that are Meridian's to make, and the work that remains. It adds to
the proposal of 28 September and its re-evaluation of 29 September and changes
nothing in either.

The visual examples are in [`2026-10-02-media-text-and-recipe-parity/examples/`](2026-10-02-media-text-and-recipe-parity/examples/index.html),
the before and after captures in [`captures/`](2026-10-02-media-text-and-recipe-parity/captures/README.md),
and the task list in [`tasks.md`](2026-10-02-media-text-and-recipe-parity/tasks.md)
(also rendered as [`examples/tasks.html`](2026-10-02-media-text-and-recipe-parity/examples/tasks.html)).
Crystal React's half, with its implementation and migration guide, is
`docs/proposals/2026-10-02-media-text-and-recipe-parity.md` in that repository.

## The brief

Meridian asked for five things, in their words:

1. There are not nearly enough component recipes to keep Crystal consistent
   across platforms, and the component libraries for other platforms will
   follow Crystal React.
2. Many animations are hidden.
3. The material hierarchy and composition are inconsistent across Crystal
   React's components.
4. Sub-elements of the audio and video players are missing their padding, and
   the players are missing features: subtitles, audio and video track
   switching, aspect ratio, playback speed.
5. The text element lacks headings, lists, checklists, underline,
   strikethrough and other text insertions, an intelligent cursor, and touch
   support.

## 1. Findings

Each finding says how it was measured. "Static" means read from source;
"rendered" means measured in a browser. The re-evaluation's lesson stands: the
rendering decides, and a static finding is a place to look.

### 1.1 Recipes across platforms

**F-1. A surface is a CSS selector, and no other platform can wear one.**
Rendered and static. `surfaces.json` ties each of the 23 surfaces to a class in
`crystal.css`. A SwiftUI or Compose library has to read the stylesheet to learn
which tokens a recipe uses, which is the position Crystal React was in before
2.2.0 (re-evaluation, recommendation 2). **Built:** `core/tokens/surface-recipes.json`,
generated by `tools/build-surface-recipes.cjs` from the stylesheet itself, gives
each surface's fill, blur, saturation, rim, shadow, radius, padding, feathered
layer, pseudo-element layers and its four fallbacks (opaque, reduced
transparency, forced colours, no backdrop filter) as token references. It is
exported as `@crystal-ui/core/surface-recipes`, and a contract test fails when
it is stale or references a token that does not exist. 19 surfaces resolve
fully, 1 is `none`, and 3 are partial for reasons the next finding gives.

**F-2. Generating the recipes found nine places where the stylesheet does not
say what the vocabulary says.** Static, from the generator's cascade
resolution. The first two were reported as accessibility defects; measured in
Chromium with forced colours emulated, the browser's own adjustment already
draws the border in a system colour and drops the shadows, and only the
`backdrop-filter` survives (D-36). The other seven stand as read:

| # | What | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `.cr-status` was read as keeping its rim border under forced colours. Rendered: the border is a system colour; only `blur(20px) saturate(1.65)` survives. | Minor (D-36) |
| 2 | Checkboxes, radios and the switch were read as keeping blur, rim and shadow. Rendered: native controls, no shadow; only `blur(20px)` survives. | Minor (D-36) |
| 3 | `.cr-button` on an element other than `button` or `a` gets no Resin coat. | Gap |
| 4 | `.cr-control` and a bare `button` have no radius; the pill comes only with `.cr-button`. | Gap |
| 5 | `.cr-stone` sets no radius. | Gap |
| 6 | `.cr-nav-item` is listed as Plastic and paints nothing (its prose says it has no material). | Vocabulary |
| 7 | `.cr-dock-inner` wears the Stone fill; the dock's entry speaks of Haze. | Vocabulary |
| 8 | `.cr-dock` and the choice controls have no `-webkit-backdrop-filter`, and the choice controls use `blur(20px)` where every other surface uses a token. | Gap |
| 9 | `.cr-haze`, `.cr-bubble` and `.cr-dialog` declare one border colour in the shorthand and a later rule replaces it. | Legibility |

**F-3. The media players and the editor had no recipe at all.** Every platform
would have to invent the stage, the transport's insets, the caption backing,
the settings menu, the editor's document styles and the touch toolbar.
**Built:** section 2.1.

### 1.2 Hidden animations

**F-4. Six recipes and four presets are played by no component.** Static, from
the manifest, re-scanned by `scripts/audit-recipes.mjs`:

| Not played | Why |
|---|---|
| `view-push-out` | The view stack plays a pop as a mirrored push (checked in `verify:behaviour`), so the recipe exists and its movement is made, but no component names it. |
| `resin-confluence`, `frost-unfold`, `plastic-settle`, `haze-tide`, `stone-contour` | The five material compositions. Each describes itself as "a visual study, not an application action". |
| Presets `plastic`, `resin`, `haze`, `stone` | Reachable through `Transition`, which takes any preset and plays `frost` by default; no catalogue component uses them. |

Neither the library nor its Storybook had anywhere these could be seen.
**Built:** `Foundations/Motion catalogue` in Crystal React's Storybook plays all
59 recipes and all 8 presets on demand, each on its own material, and
[`examples/motion.html`](2026-10-02-media-text-and-recipe-parity/examples/motion.html)
does the same from the core package alone. Whether the five presets should
become entrances for surfaces a person opens is D-31.

**F-5. 20 assigned motions are played by nothing, across 15 entries.** Static.
Crystal React's manifest credited a component only with what its own source
plays, so a menu's `menu-in` inside a menubar counted as missing. **Built:**
`composedRecipes` in the manifest credits what a component renders, one level
down (re-evaluation, recommendation 5). With it, 278 of 338 assignments are
played by the component and 40 by a child. The 20 that remain fall into the
three groups the re-evaluation named:

| Group | Entries | What it needs |
|---|---|---|
| The component cannot play it | `list-out` on the transfer list, the data table and the resizable table; `accordion-out` on the tree view, navigation tree, organisation chart and spoiler; `page-out` on master and detail; `list-in` on the combobox | A ruling as D-28 gave: take it off, or say what the component must grow (D-32). |
| The product's child plays it | `menu-in`/`menu-out` on the menubar and the split button, whose menus are the product's children | Catalogue prose saying so (D-32). |
| A real gap | The cascader's field recipes; `media-in` on the product gallery; `list-in`/`list-out` on the playlist block; `view-push-out` | Crystal React tasks R-A3 to R-A5. |

### 1.3 Material hierarchy and composition in Crystal React

**F-6. 29 of 237 component directories paint `backdrop-filter` by hand (79
declarations), and 49 use the library's material mixins** (`haze-fill` 25,
`field.shell` 15, `frost` 8, `resin` 3). Static, deduplicated by directory.
Each is either a surface the component should wear or a composition the
vocabulary lacks; the full list is in Crystal React's `audit.json`, and sorting
it is Crystal React task R-A1 (re-evaluation, recommendation 6).

**F-7. Four media and text components were measured and corrected.** Rendered,
before and after captures in `captures/`:

| Component | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Audio player | No surface. The transport floated on the page; the title had no ground; the play button sat flush with the pill's edge. | A Haze card with the 20px card padding, holding the title, a second line and the transport. |
| Media controls | Time readouts in muted ink straight on 20% Resin over a moving picture; the scrubber's thumb overlapping `0:00` at the start. | Readouts on Haze pills in text ink; both sliders inset by half a thumb. |
| Video player | No aspect ratio, so the stage collapsed to the browser's 300×150 until metadata arrived; transport 8px from a 28px corner. | 16:9 by default; transport inset 12px; captions on Stone above it. |
| Rich text surface | A hand-written copy of Resin and its own Haze, drifting from `.cr-field-shell`. | Wears `.cr-field-shell`; the hand-written frame is gone. |

### 1.4 The media players

**F-8. Missing features.** Static and rendered: no subtitle language choice
(only an on/off toggle), no audio-track choice, no quality choice, no playback
speed, no aspect ratio, no picture in picture, and no full screen outside the
player shell. Captions were drawn by the browser at the foot of the picture,
under the transport whenever it showed. The story clip was an MP4 header with
no frames, so no story had shown a picture, a cue or a duration.

### 1.5 The text element

**F-9. The rich text surface held no vocabulary.** It took an engine from the
product and styled none of what an engine produces: headings, lists,
checklists, quotations, code, underline, strikethrough, insertions,
deletions, highlights, sub- and superscript. Its own stories offered Bold and
Italic. There was no touch behaviour: a phone keyboard has no Tab, Ctrl+B or
Ctrl+Z, so a list could not be nested or a change undone, and nothing kept the
toolbar clear of the on-screen keyboard.

**F-10. Crystal's icon set lacks the editor's glyphs.** Static.
`tools/build-icons.cjs` keeps the first 1000 icons alphabetically after its
filters, so `link`, `list`, `list-ordered`, `quote`, `redo`, `subscript` and
`undo` fall past the cap, and the `-digit` filter drops `heading-2` to
`heading-4`. Filed as D-35.

### 1.6 Selection in strips and groups

**F-11. A selection never travels.** Static and rendered; raised after the
proposal, on 2 October. Tabs, the segmented control, the dock, bottom navigation
and toggle button groups paint selection on each item, so changing it switches
one fill off and another on. The only motion is `selection`'s squash in place,
and tabs play `tab-in` on the panel, not the tab. `surfaces.json` describes "the
moving pill behind a selected segment" under `.cr-indicator`; `crystal.css`
draws no such pill and no recipe moves a selection between positions. Crystal
React plays every assignment these components have. Filed as D-37.

## 2. What was built

### 2.1 Recipes in core (`crystal.css`, after 2.3.1)

One block, "Media and text recipes", in `@layer crystal.component`: ten recipes, each
a composition of existing surfaces with its own opaque, reduced-transparency
and forced-colours branches. No surface was added; four selectors were
registered as `also` on existing ones.

| Selector | Surface | What it fixes |
|---|---|---|
| `.cr-media` | (stage) | Content radius, letterbox, `--cr-media-aspect`, `--cr-media-fit`, container for the transport's narrow layout, `--cr-media-transport` for the caption to clear. |
| `.cr-media-bar` | (region) | The transport inset 12px, or the safe area in full screen. |
| `.cr-resin.transport` | resin | 4px block and 12px inline padding around the 48px targets (58px with its rim); tabular readouts on Haze pills; wraps below 420px. |
| `.cr-media-caption`, `.cr-media video::cue` | stone | A cue on feathered Stone, above the transport; the flat fill for the browser's own renderer. |
| `.cr-haze.cr-media.audio` | haze + resin | The audio card: padding, gap, and `background: revert-layer` so the stage's letterbox does not paint over Haze. |
| `.cr-frost.bar` | frost | A transient overlay shaped as a bar of controls: the selection toolbar. |
| `.cr-prose`, `.cr-editor` | (typography), field | One reading vocabulary, with the values Crystal React's `Prose` renders, plus checklists, `u`, `s`, `ins`, `del`, `kbd`, `sub`, `sup`; the editor's primary caret and a 28% primary selection tint that stays distinct from a highlight. |
| `.cr-editor-toolbar`, `.cr-editor-toolbar.below` | field | Above the text on a fine pointer; below it and sticky above `keyboard-inset-height` on a coarse one, or anywhere with `.below`. |
| `.cr-field-shell:has(> .cr-editor)` | field | The editor's frame: the shell as a column at the content radius, padded by its Haze inset. |

A contract check holds the geometry: the insets, the transport height, the
Haze readouts, the Stone caption above the bar, `revert-layer` on the audio
card, no check glyph anywhere in the block, no strikethrough on a finished
checklist item, the keyboard inset, the four registrations, and that nothing
in the block is in the reset layer.

### 2.2 The catalogue

`tools/extend-catalogue-8.cjs` changes 38 fields across ten entries and adds
none, so the count stays 284 and no platform gains a component it must build.
Every old value is quoted in the tool, and it refuses to write on a value it
does not recognise.

| Entry | Gains |
|---|---|
| `video-player` | The stage, settings menu, captions on Stone, picture in picture and full screen; states `captions-on`, `settings-open`, `picture-in-picture`, `full-screen`; surfaces `resin, haze, stone, frost`; semantics for radio-item menus with selection by weight; `menu-in`/`menu-out`. |
| `audio-player` | The Haze card and the speed menu; surfaces `haze, resin, frost`. |
| `media-controls` | Geometry with the insets; Haze behind the readouts. |
| `player-shell` | Track and speed choices announced. |
| `rich-text-surface` | The format vocabulary, the selection toolbar, touch placement; states `selection-toolbar-open`, `touch-keyboard-open`; surfaces `field, frost`; the field recipes and the menu recipes, which it now plays. |
| `editor-block`, `prose`, `prose-list`, `text` | The same vocabulary in read-only form: checklists, decorations, insertions and deletions. |

### 2.3 Crystal React

In summary; the implementation and migration guide is Crystal React's
proposal.

- **Media.** `useMediaElement` gains playback rate and picture in picture;
  `useMediaTracks` reads and switches caption, audio and video tracks on the
  element, offering only what the engine can switch. `MediaSettings` is one
  menu of radio items, a submenu per choice when there are several, selection
  by weight, every change announced. `VideoPlayer` gains `aspectRatio`, `fit`,
  `playbackRates`, `qualities`, `nativeCaptions`, picture in picture, full
  screen and the shortcuts C, F, Shift+< and Shift+>, draws its own caption
  cues on Stone, and portals its menus into itself in full screen.
  `AudioPlayer` is a Haze card with `subtitle` and a speed menu.
- **Text.** `RichTextSurface` wears `.cr-field-shell`, carries the reading
  vocabulary (shared with `Prose` through one mixin), plays the field recipes,
  takes a `toolbar` slot, and places the toolbar by pointer, above the
  keyboard on touch. The format vocabulary is exported from the main entry,
  engine-agnostic. `RichTextEditor`, from the new `@crystal-ui/react/editor`
  entry with TipTap as an optional peer dependency, binds TipTap 3 to it
  (D-30).
- **Motion.** The motion catalogue story; `composedRecipes` in the manifest.
- **Gates.** A version guard that fails when core publishes the recipes this
  library still carries copies of; `scripts/audit-recipes.mjs`; real-browser
  checks for the caption cue, the transport geometry, the settings menu and
  the editor's touch layout; a 120 KB generated clip with two audio tracks and
  captions in two languages.

## 3. Decisions for Meridian

Each is filed in [`open-issues.md`](open-issues.md) with its options. The
recommendation is marked. Meridian rules on them on the tasks page
([`examples/tasks.html`](2026-10-02-media-text-and-recipe-parity/examples/tasks.html), served by
`examples/serve.cjs`): a ruling is appended to `rulings.json`, written to the record
[`2026-10-02-rulings.md`](2026-10-02-rulings.md) and to its `open-issues.md` entry, and the
tasks it unblocks, drops or adds change status on the board, in `tasks.md` and, after
`pnpm run sync:slice-r`, in Crystal React's Slice R. A decision that relies on another (D-33
on D-30) can be ruled only after it.

| | Question | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| D-30 | Should a platform library ship an optional engine binding for the rich text surface? Crystal React now does, at `@crystal-ui/react/editor`, on the strength of Meridian's brief. | Yes, as an opt-in entry point with the engine as an optional peer, and the catalogue says the binding is optional. It can be removed without breaking anything else. |
| D-31 | Should the material presets become entrances for surfaces a person opens (Frost for a side sheet, Resin for a floating transport)? `presets.js` calls them entrance movement; `motion.md` calls them studies. | Keep them as studies in 2.x, made visible (built), and decide entrances per surface in 3.0 against the approved baseline. |
| D-32 | The 20 unplayed assignments: which come off, and which components grow? | As D-28: take off `list-out` on the three tables, `accordion-out` on the four trees and the spoiler, `page-out` on master and detail; say in prose that the menubar's and split button's menus are the product's; keep the rest as React tasks. |
| D-33 | What did "intelligent cursor" mean? | Confirm the reading built: formatting state follows the caret, a gap cursor between non-text blocks, a drop cursor, Markdown input rules, shortcut changes announced, Alt+F10 to the toolbar. |
| D-34 | A highlight (`mark`) and the editor's selection both used primary-soft. | Keep `mark` on primary-soft as `Prose` renders it, and keep the selection on the 28% primary tint (built). The alternative is the attention pair for `mark`, a visible change to `Prose`. |
| D-35 | The icon set's 1000-icon cap drops the editor's glyphs. | Add a named list of icons the vocabulary requires, kept whatever the cap, and rebuild; disclose every icon that leaves the set. |
| D-37 | Should a selection travel between segments: tabs, segmented controls, the dock, bottom navigation, toggle button groups? | A travelling `.cr-indicator.pill` on every strip, moved by a new critically damped recipe between measured positions, instant under reduced motion, label weight unchanged. A visible change, so it waits on the ruling. |
| D-36 | A backdrop blur that survives forced colours on the status badge and the choices (static report corrected by measurement). | Order `backdrop-filter: none` after the base rules and check it by measuring, in 2.4.0. No aesthetic change. |

## 5. Release

The recipes reach Crystal React only from a published core. Until then Crystal
React carries their values in its own stylesheets, and
`src/media/coreRecipes.test.ts` fails on the day the installed core publishes
them, as R-20's guard did for 2.2.0. The sequence is task C-R1 to C-R3 and
R-M9: publish 2.4.0, bump the range, wear the classes, delete the copies.
