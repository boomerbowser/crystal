A bolder palette, with guardrails.

  • Expressive colors live in a separate brand namespace.
  • Content fills blend at 80%; text and input boundaries stay crisp.
  • Resin labels have a protected tint behind them.
  • Check marks mean validated or informational, never selected.
  • Selections use a leading rail and label weight, not just hue.
  • Reduced transparency, reduced motion, and forced colors preserve the interface.

WCAG does not reserve red, green, amber, or any other hue. It requires sufficient contrast and visible information beyond color. Read the W3C guidance.
